⚡ Kamus Lengkap GvRng

                

                  Instruksi bawaan Guido van Robot NG (GvRng) sesuai modul praktikum UMS:
                

                
                
1. Gerak Dasar:

                
    
                  
  • move — Maju 1 langkah ke depan
    • 
                  
  • turnleft — Belok 90° ke kiri
    • 
                  
  • pickbeeper — Ambil 1 beeper dari petak robot
    • 
                  
  • putbeeper — Taruh 1 beeper dari tas robot
    • 
                  
  • turnoff — Matikan robot (mengakhiri program)
    • 
                


                
2. Sensor Dinding & Rintangan:

                
    
                  
  • front_is_clear / front_is_blocked
    • 
                  
  • left_is_clear / left_is_blocked
    • 
                  
  • right_is_clear / right_is_blocked
    • 
                


                
3. Sensor Beeper & Arah:

                
    
                  
  • next_to_a_beeper / not_next_to_a_beeper
    • 
                  
  • any_beepers_in_beeper_bag / no_beepers_in_beeper_bag
    • 
                  
  • facing_north, facing_south, facing_east, facing_west
    •
