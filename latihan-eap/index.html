Panduan Lengkap: Simple, Compound (FANBOYS), & Complex Sentences

â–¼

Dalam penulisan akademik bahasa Inggris, variasi struktur kalimat sangat menentukan kejelasan dan kekuatan argumen. Kalimat diklasifikasikan berdasarkan jumlah dan jenis klausa (Independent vs Dependent Clause) yang menyusunnya:

1. SIMPLE SENTENCE (Kalimat Tunggal)

1 Independent Clause

Hanya memiliki 1 Independent Clause (klausa bebas lengkap dengan Subject dan Finite Verb yang dapat berdiri sendiri sebagai gagasan utuh).

Pola Dasar: [Subject] + [Verb] (+ Object / Complement / Adverbial)
Independent Clause
The internet helps students to find information quickly.
*Bedah Komponen: Subject: The internet | Verb: helps | Object: students | Adverbial: to find information quickly.
Independent Clause (Subjek Majemuk)
Both software engineers and data analysts tested the neural network.
*Catatan: Subjek majemuk (*compound subject*) tetap membentuk Simple Sentence selama hanya ada 1 predikat utama (*finite verb*).

2. COMPOUND SENTENCE (Kalimat Majemuk Setara)

2+ Independent Clauses + FANBOYS

Menggabungkan 2 atau lebih Independent Clauses yang memiliki bobot gramatikal setara. Kedua klausa dihubungkan oleh Coordinating Conjunction (FANBOYS) dan WAJIB didahului tanda koma (,).

Rumus Wajib Tanda Koma:
[Independent Clause] , FANBOYS [Independent Clause]
Independent 1 Independent 2
The autonomous drone returned to base, for its primary battery depleted below ten percent.
*Konjungsi for berarti *karena/sebab* (*because*). Perhatikan tanda koma persis sebelum kata *for*.
I like programming, and I also enjoy designing intuitive user interfaces.
The central server crashed, so the technician rebooted the system immediately.
ðŸ“‹ Tabel Sakti 7 Konjungsi FANBOYS (Standar Teknik Informatika):
Huruf Konjungsi Fungsi Logis / Semantik Contoh Kalimat Akademik Informatika
F For Menyatakan alasan / sebab (because) The autonomous drone returned to base, for its primary battery depleted below ten percent.
A And Menambahkan ide yang sejalan (addition) I like programming, and I also enjoy designing intuitive user interfaces.
N Nor Menyatakan penyangkalan ganda negatif The server did not crash during peak hours, nor did it corrupt any database records.
B But Menyatakan pertentangan / kontras langsung I wanted to purchase an enterprise server, but the licensing fee was too expensive.
O Or Menyajikan alternatif / pilihan Students must update their security credentials, or the system will terminate access.
Y Yet Kontras yang mengejutkan (nevertheless) The sorting algorithm was mathematically complex, yet it executed in under two milliseconds.
S So Menyatakan hasil / konsekuensi logis The central server crashed, so the technician rebooted the system immediately.

3. COMPLEX SENTENCE (Kalimat Majemuk Bertingkat)

1 Indep + 1+ Dep Clause

Terdiri dari 1 Independent Clause + minimal 1 Dependent Clause (klausa terikat yang diawali oleh Subordinating Conjunction).

â€¢ Subordinator Umum: because although even though since while whereas if unless after before when until.

Independent (Depan) She stayed at home because Dependent it was raining heavily.

4. COMPOUND-COMPLEX SENTENCE

Min. 2 Indep + 1 Dep Clause

Menggabungkan minimal 2 Independent Clauses dan minimal 1 Dependent Clause dalam satu kesatuan argumen yang padu.

Indep 1 Indep 2 Dep Clause
The students were exhausted, but they continued developing the application because the competition deadline was approaching.

Sentence Traps
